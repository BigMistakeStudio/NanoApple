# Propositions d’icône NanoApple

Trois concepts générés avec l’outil imagegen intégré. Le concept 1, « Pomme cadrée », est installé dans `NanoApple/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`.

## 1. Pomme cadrée

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: concept for a macOS app icon for NanoApple, an offline AI image creation app. Create one finished 1024x1024 square app icon proposal, visually pristine at 32px. Concept A, 'Pomme cadrée': a highly simplified, original apple-like fruit outline (not the Apple Inc logo) made from one continuous thick warm-white geometric ribbon; inside it, a small simple square picture frame indicated by a single corner and a small sun circle. One tiny leaf above. Deep midnight blue rounded-square tile with a subtle matte gradient, no neon, no glow, no chrome, no 3D, no textures, no text, no letters, no border, no extra icons. Strong negative space, precise alignment, generously centered, unmistakable silhouette at small size. Transparent outside the rounded-square tile.

## 2. Iris

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one finished 1024x1024 macOS app icon proposal for NanoApple, an offline AI image creation app. Concept B, 'Iris': an original circular camera aperture mark made from exactly five broad flat curved blades, opening as a clean pentagonal negative space; a single small detached leaf-shaped accent above-right subtly alludes to the app name without drawing an apple silhouette. Center the mark on a warm near-black charcoal rounded-square tile. Aperture blades in soft pale silver, one blade in muted teal; leaf in same muted teal. Ultra-simple modern Swiss graphic design, precise geometry, high contrast at 32px, generous margins. Flat matte colors only, no glow, no 3D, no gradients, no text, no letters, no extra objects, no trademark logo. Transparent outside the rounded-square tile.

## 3. Pixel en image

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one finished 1024x1024 macOS app icon proposal for NanoApple, an offline AI image creation app. Concept C, 'Pixel en image': a single original abstract fruit mark, made of a smooth solid coral-red rounded fruit shape with one small detached pale leaf, with exactly three small square pixels cleanly dissolving from its left edge; the fruit must feel like a simple creative-image symbol rather than any existing corporate logo. Center it on a very dark plum rounded-square tile. Flat graphic design, only coral, pale cream, and dark plum; no outlines, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no texture, no text, no letters, no extra objects. Bold silhouette, wide negative space, legible at 32px. Transparent outside the rounded-square tile.

Les propositions originales mesurent 1254 × 1254 px. Le concept retenu a été décliné dans les dix tailles macOS de l’AppIcon Xcode.
