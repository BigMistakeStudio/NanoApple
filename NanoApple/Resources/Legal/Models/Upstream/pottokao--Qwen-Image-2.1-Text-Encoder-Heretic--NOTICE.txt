Qwen-Image-2.1 Text Encoder - Heretic

Qwen-Image-2.1 uses this same model, unmodified, as its text encoder; the weights
here match Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct except for the modifications below.

Modifications from the original:
  - Refusal directions removed via directional ablation (Heretic,
    https://github.com/p-e-w/heretic). Affected tensors: self_attn.o_proj and
    mlp.down_proj in a subset of layers; all other weights are unchanged.
